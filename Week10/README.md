## 💭 Exit Reflection

The most significant skill I developed across these ten weeks is understanding that a trained model is only one part of a complete ML system. Building the recommendation engine for دلني — from collecting and cleaning data manually, through designing the scoring pipeline, to deploying the service — showed how much work sits between a working notebook and something real users can actually use.

The part I am proudest of is the dataset itself. Researching Palestinian tourist attractions, verifying coordinates, and writing descriptions in both Arabic and English felt meaningful beyond the technical work.

**Next steps:** Fine-tune MobileNetV2 for the melanoma classifier, explore collaborative filtering to extend دلني beyond content-based matching, and continue building in public on GitHub.
